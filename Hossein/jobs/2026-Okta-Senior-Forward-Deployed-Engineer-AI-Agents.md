## About Okta for AI Agents

Okta secures access for 20,000 organizations and billions of users. Okta for AI Agents extends that work to the agentic shift.

Deploying an AI agent is not like deploying traditional software. It puts professional work output into production and therefore requires deep integration, continuous tuning, and organizational change management. Every agent needs an identity, an authorized scope, an audit trail, and a way to be shut down when it behaves incorrectly. Most enterprises have not built this foundation yet. Okta is building it.

The team hires builders who recognize unresolved cracks in enterprise agent identity and are motivated to solve them.

## The Role

The Senior Forward Deployed Engineer embeds inside four to five of Okta’s most strategic enterprise customers as their dedicated technical partner for agent identity. The engineer works alongside customer identity, platform, and security engineering teams, writes production code in the customer environment, and owns the technical outcome from prototype through production.

This is a builder-consultant role. The engineer goes beyond architecture diagrams to code, debug, and ship bespoke agent identity solutions inside customer environments. The role is responsible for helping customers ship secure agents faster while feeding field insights back to Okta product engineering.

- Become the customer’s trusted technical voice on agent security. Participate in standups, design reviews, and incident response. Earn a seat on architecture review boards and security councils for agent-risk decisions.
- Architect and deploy with the customer’s team. Integrate Okta’s agent-security stack into customer infrastructure, including Cross-App Access (XAA), Fine-Grained Authorization (FGA), MCP Gateway, and agent client registration.
- Own identity, delegation, audit, and kill-switch architecture end to end, and coach customer engineers on the relevant patterns.
- Engage senior leadership, including CISOs, CIOs, identity leaders, Chief AI Officers, and principal architects.
- Translate token-exchange flows into board-level agent risk, and translate AI-governance mandates into implementable architecture.
- Deliver white-glove deployments resulting in production agents with complete identity coverage, successful security review, satisfied governance requirements, and online posture visibility.
- Keep deployments defensible by aligning architecture decisions with the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, NIST AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS, and applicable regulatory or assurance frameworks such as HIPAA, FedRAMP, and SOC 2.
- Integrate Okta for AI Agents with customer identity providers for human-to-agent links, IGA systems for agent lifecycle, ISPM systems for posture, SIEM and EDR platforms for behavioral coverage, and policy engines for runtime decisions.
- Build evaluations and observability for authorization-decision latency, agent scope sprawl, anomalous delegation chains, audit completeness, kill-switch verification, and rogue-agent detection.
- Extract recurring field gaps from customer architects and governance leaders and convert them into reusable modules and product-roadmap improvements.
- Maintain regular on-site presence at customer locations to build trust and align governance and architecture decisions.
