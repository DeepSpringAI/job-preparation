# Recruiter Prescreen — What Makes Me Different?

## Candidate's natural differentiators

1. **Hands-on technical judgment**
   - Does not rely on social-media narratives or vendor claims.
   - Tests new models, tools, coding agents, and harnesses against real tasks before adopting them.

2. **Stays current without chasing hype**
   - Actively follows new AI capabilities.
   - Evaluates them empirically rather than assuming newer automatically means better.

3. **Business/workflow orientation**
   - Believes many AI projects fail because teams rush to implementation before understanding the real business process.
   - Works backward from the user's workflow, decision criteria, constraints, and measurable outcome.

## Coaching adjustment

Avoid cliché framing like:
- "I work hard"
- "I am a fast learner"
- "I keep up with technology"

Make each differentiator observable and specific.

## Recruiter-ready answer

"I'd probably point to three things.

First, I'm very hands-on. Even at a senior level, I don't like making technical decisions based on what is popular or what people are saying online. When a new model, agent framework, coding tool, or architecture appears, I usually test it myself against realistic tasks and failure cases before deciding whether it belongs in a production system.

Second, I try to stay current without being driven by hype. In AI the tooling changes extremely quickly, so I think good judgment comes from continuously experimenting and understanding where a new capability is actually better, where it isn't, and what operational tradeoffs it introduces.

Third, and probably most importantly, I tend to start from the business workflow rather than the model. I've seen many technically impressive AI projects fail because the team optimized the implementation before deeply understanding what decision the user was trying to make, what information they trusted, and where the real bottleneck was.

That combination—hands-on technical depth, disciplined evaluation of new AI capabilities, and a strong focus on the underlying business workflow—is probably what differentiates me most. It lets me move from an ambiguous problem to a production system without getting trapped either in pure research or in building the wrong thing very efficiently."

## Core message

**I test new technology myself, separate signal from hype, and start from the real business decision rather than the model.**
