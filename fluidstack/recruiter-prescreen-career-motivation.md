# Recruiter Prescreen — What I Want Next / Why This Role

## Candidate thesis

AI coding systems are changing software and ML engineering by dramatically increasing implementation leverage. As implementation becomes faster, more of the engineer's differentiation moves toward:

- identifying the right problem
- observing real workflows
- understanding domain constraints
- translating ambiguous expert judgment into explicit system behavior
- designing the human/AI/software boundary
- validating output and owning production outcomes

## Important interview adjustment

Avoid saying:
- AI can now almost fully do implementation
- coding is no longer important
- this will be the future job of all software engineers

For Fluidstack, those statements are too absolute because the Decision Engineer role explicitly expects engineers to ship production software while using AI coding tools and agents heavily.

Better framing:
> "AI is making implementation much more leveraged, which makes problem selection, workflow understanding, system design, and validation even more important. I still want to be hands-on, but I want to work closer to the domain problem and own the path from ambiguity to production."

## Recruiter-ready answer

"What I'm looking for next is a role where I can stay very hands-on technically but work much closer to the actual business problem.

I think AI is changing the engineering role quite significantly. Implementation is becoming much more leveraged because coding agents can help us explore solutions, generate code, test ideas, and move much faster than before. That doesn't make engineering less important; it shifts more of the value toward identifying the right problem, understanding the real workflow, designing the system boundaries and controls, and validating that what we built actually solves the problem.

That was actually the part of my work at Intapp that I enjoyed most. The most interesting problems weren't just model-selection or implementation problems. They came from sitting with users, understanding how investment professionals actually worked, translating that into a system, and then iterating based on what happened in production.

What attracts me to Decision Engineering is that Fluidstack has made that way of working the core of the role: embed with domain experts, understand a high-value operational problem, and own the solution end to end while using AI to move extremely quickly.

Intapp gave me a strong foundation in that style of work. At this point, I want my next role to make it the center of what I do rather than one part of a broader product organization."

## Core message

**Stay hands-on, move closer to the problem, use AI as leverage, own the outcome.**
